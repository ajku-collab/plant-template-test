Neem Tree

Azadirachta indica

Taxonomy

Family: Meliaceae

Genus: Azadirachta

Species: A. indica

Growth Conditions

Soil: Well-drained sandy or clay soils

Water: Drought-tolerant, requires minimal irrigation

Sunlight: Full sun exposure

Climate: Tropical and subtropical regions

Medicinal & Practical Uses

Neem leaves, bark, and oil are widely used in traditional medicine for their antibacterial, antifungal, and anti-inflammatory properties. Neem oil is also used in natural pesticides and skincare products.

Conservation Status

Currently listed as a species of Least Concern due to its wide distribution and adaptability.
